Made something?

Give your app a little sunshine.

Add its package name, version, screenshots or links, and a short description. Sign in to submit an APK for review before it appears in the public garden.

Share responsibly

Only upload apps you own or have permission to distribute. Include accurate details, and never bundle anything deceptive or harmful.

Make details useful

Use the exact Android package ID and version, explain what the app does, and link to the source or project page when possible.

Review before release

New submissions start in a review queue. Review helps reduce spam and gives visitors more confidence before downloading an APK.
